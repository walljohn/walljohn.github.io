About

A Cloyne business changing hands, not changing direction

Ron Nowell Construction is based in Cloyne, Ontario, working in and around North Frontenac. Ron Nowell built the business doing the kind of ground work rural properties and cottages need — excavation, septic systems, driveways, and gravel.

In 2024, Ron began preparing to retire. Rather than close up shop, his son Ryan Nowell and the crew — including Jamie Miles — took over day-to-day operations, running things as usual. Ron is still around to help out when the work calls for it.

It is a small, local operation: the people who answer the phone are the same people who show up to run the equipment.
