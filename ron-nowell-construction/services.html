Services

What Ron Nowell Construction takes on

These are the jobs this business has long advertised in the Cloyne area. Offerings can change with the season and crew capacity, so confirm details with Ryan before you plan around them.

Excavation & Site Prep

This is the ground-floor work: clearing a lot, digging footings, and grading a pad so a foundation or addition has something solid to sit on.

Ryan and the crew handle site prep for new builds, cottage additions, and outbuildings around Cloyne — moving the earth so the next trade can start clean.

Septic Systems

A lot of properties around Cloyne and North Frontenac run on a septic system rather than municipal sewer, and installing one right — tank, bed, and grading — is part of what this business has long been known for.

If you're building new, replacing an aging system, or not sure what your lot needs, call Ryan to talk through it.

Roads & Driveways

Getting a vehicle to a rural property or cottage often means building the road first. That covers laneways, shared cottage roads, and driveway construction — cutting grade, placing base material, and setting culverts where water needs to cross.

Existing driveways that have gotten rutted or soft can usually be reworked rather than rebuilt from scratch.

Sand, Gravel & Fill

Whether it's fill to level a low spot, gravel for a driveway top-up, or sand for a beach or play area, this is delivered and spread on-site rather than left in a pile for you to deal with.

Useful on its own or as part of a bigger excavation or driveway job.

Snowplowing

Comes winter, the same equipment that does site work goes on plow duty — keeping driveways and private roads clear so you can get in and out.

Ask Ryan about current winter availability in your area before the snow flies.

Not sure which service fits?

Tell Ryan what you are trying to get done and where — he will point you to the right service, or tell you it is not something he can take on.
