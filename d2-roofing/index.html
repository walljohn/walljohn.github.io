Not Sure If You Need a New Roof?

Signs Your Roof Needs Attention

Cracked or Curled Shingles

Dry, brittle shingles that crack or break when touched have reached the end of their useful life.

Missing Shingles

Gaps leave your roof susceptible to leaks — the next rainstorm could cause real damage.

Leak in the Attic

Often caused by ice dams, deteriorating flashing, or worn underlayment.

Stains on Ceilings or Walls

A sign of mold, mildew, or underlayment failure that shouldn't be ignored.
