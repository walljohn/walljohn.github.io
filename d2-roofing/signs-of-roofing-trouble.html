Do You Need a New Roof?

Signs of Roofing Trouble

Look for these tell-tale signs. If you notice any of them, contact us as soon as possible.

Sign 1 of 8

Cracked or Curled Shingles

Probably the most common sign that a home needs a new roof is cracked or curled shingles. If your shingles are dry or crack when touched, they've reached the end of their useful life and it's time to consider a new roof.

Sign 2 of 8

Missing Shingles

Missing shingles leave your roof susceptible to leaks — the next rainstorm could be catastrophic, with excessive damage to your ceilings and walls a real risk.

Sign 3 of 8

A Leak in the Attic

Attic leaks are often caused by ice dams, deteriorating flashing, or inadequate underlayment.

Sign 4 of 8

Blistering or Peeling Paint

Blistering or peeling paint, inside or outside the home, can be caused by poor attic ventilation and excess humidity.

Sign 5 of 8

Dark, "Dirty-Looking" Areas on Your Roof

Dark streaks can be caused by vegetation, fungus, mold, or algae — or by shingles that have lost their protective granules.

Sign 6 of 8

Exterior Decay on Sheathing or Siding

Decay on exterior sheathing or siding is often a sign of moisture getting where it shouldn't — a proper vapour barrier can help prevent it.

Sign 7 of 8

Stains on Interior Ceilings and Walls

Stains on interior ceilings or walls can point to mold or mildew caused by underlayment failure or ventilation issues.

Sign 8 of 8

Excessive Energy Costs

Rising heating and cooling costs are often tied to insufficient attic ventilation.

Noticing Any of These Signs?

Contact us as soon as possible — we'll follow up to take a look.
