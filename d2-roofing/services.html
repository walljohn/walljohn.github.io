Asphalt Shingles, Steel & Cedar Shakes

Whether it's a residential project — home, garage, or outbuilding — or a commercial building, our crews are experienced across all four roofing types below.

Most Popular

Asphalt Shingles

Asphalt shingles are divided into three categories:

  • Three-Tab Shingles — the basic, most economical type of asphalt shingle.
  • Laminated (Architectural) Shingles — layered construction gives these a thicker, more dimensional look than three-tab shingles.
  • Designer Shingles — an upscale appearance in both colour and shape.

We can advise on the best shingle option for your home, including IKO Dynasty — see our preferred shingle.

Steel Panels & Steel Shingles

A metal roof is a roofing system made from metal pieces or tiles, characterized by high resistance, impermeability, and longevity.

Steel is the most common metal roofing material — sturdy, with manufacturers using zinc coatings, sealants, and heavy-duty paint to protect it from rust and the elements. We install both steel panel and steel shingle systems.

Cedar Shakes

A shake is a rustic-looking roofing shingle, hand-split to replicate the look of an axe or mallet cut — rough, variable, and almost always used for roofing.

  • Naturally beautiful, with real curb appeal
  • Extremely durable and of the highest quality
  • 100% sustainable and natural
  • Significantly more environmentally friendly than asphalt shingles
  • Random widths for natural variety
