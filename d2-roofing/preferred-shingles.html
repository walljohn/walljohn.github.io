Shingle Options

D2 Roofing Recommends IKO Dynasty

IKO Dynasty Performance Shingles

IKO Dynasty shingles are engineered to resist high winds and carry a limited wind warranty for winds up to 130 mph (210 km/h). Their standard asphalt coating is embedded with blue-green algae-resistant granules that help resist the black streaks and discolouration algae can cause.

Dynasty is manufactured in IKO's "Advantage" size — larger than many competitors' comparable products — and is available in a range of high-definition colour blends.

ArmourZone® Nailing Surface

Dynasty's reinforced ArmourZone nailing surface is designed to reduce the risk of call-backs from nails pulling through the shingle in high wind, and supports the shingle's 130 mph wind rating with a standard four-nail application.

FastLock® Sealant Tabs

Modified-bitumen FastLock sealant tabs are warmed by the sun to create a strong bond, helping shingles lay flat even in high-wind conditions.
