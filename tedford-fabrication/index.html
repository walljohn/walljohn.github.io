Tedford Fabrication

A hand-craft studio for rattan weaving, furniture restoration, decorating and painting, and metal railing repair — run personally by Carrie Tedford.

Tedford Fabrication reweaves and repairs rattan and wicker seating, restores and refinishes furniture, paints and decorates interior spaces, and repairs metal railings. Every project, from the first phone call to the finished piece, is handled by Carrie herself, right here in Gananoque.

About the maker

Trained to look. Taught to build.

Carrie Tedford studied curatorial practice at OCAD University before spending nine-plus years inside the paint-and-coatings and kitchen-and-bath trade — a combination that shows up in how she restores, finishes, and decorates every piece that comes through her studio.
