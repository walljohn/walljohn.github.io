Services

Four crafts, one set of hands

Carrie Tedford personally handles every stage of every job — quoting, sourcing materials, and finishing the work herself. These are the confirmed services Tedford Fabrication offers, grouped the way the work actually happens.

Rattan & Hand Weaving

Traditional hand weaving for cane and rattan seating, repaired strand by strand.

  • Hand weaving of rattan and cane seats
  • Wicker and rattan furniture repair
  • Custom home decor pieces made from natural rattan, using new and reclaimed materials

Furniture Restoration & Refinishing

Bringing worn or damaged furniture back, rather than replacing it.

  • Restoration of furniture in wood or metal
  • Refinishing and repair work focused on reviving existing pieces
  • Spray finishing of wood and metal furniture

Decorating, Painting & Home Decor

Interior finishing and decor styling, informed by a visual arts background.

  • Interior painting and finishing of drywall, wood, and metal surfaces
  • Interior decorating consultations and home decor styling
  • Decorative and mural painting, drawing on experience that spans canvas painting to large-scale theatre scenery

Metal Railings & Repairs

Keeping metal railings and hardware sound and finished.

  • Repair of metal railings and hardware
  • Spray finishing of metal railings and fixtures to match or refresh an existing finish
Not sure which category fits?

Most projects touch more than one of these

A restored chair often needs a new woven seat and a fresh finish. A railing repair might lead into a repaint. Describe the project as you see it — Carrie will sort out the rest.
