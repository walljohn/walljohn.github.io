About

Carrie Tedford

Curator-trained, trade-tested, and raised in Gananoque.

Carrie Tedford has been drawn to colour and to making things for as long as she can remember. Those instincts eventually became a business built entirely around hands-on craft.

In 2006, she graduated from OCAD University in Toronto with a Bachelor of Fine Arts in Curatorial Practices and Criticism. Afterward, she spent more than nine years working in building-material retail, inside the paint-and-coatings and kitchen-and-bath design departments — practical, hands-on product knowledge that now underpins the restoration, refinishing, and decorating work she does today.

“I love seeing someone's house become their home.” — Carrie Tedford

Her visual arts practice runs wider than furniture and interiors: personal board and canvas paintings, smaller residential wall murals, and work on large-scale theatre scenery all sit alongside the weaving, restoration, and finishing work that make up Tedford Fabrication.

Apart from her years in Toronto at OCAD, Carrie was born and raised in Gananoque, and returned to build her business here — in a town she describes as offering "a wonderous landscape that whole heartedly endorses the best of all seasons," within easy reach of Ottawa, Toronto, and the U.S. border.

Creator.

It's the single word Carrie uses to sum up Tedford Fabrication — because no shorter list captures weaving, restoration, paint, and metal all coming out of the same studio.
